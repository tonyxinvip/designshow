Day by Day

An old wall. Lines from the Dao De Jing, written vertically on strips of paper, are pasted onto it one after another; each new strip overlaps the ones already there, and the earliest strips are the most yellowed. When the last one goes up the wall reads 為學日益 — day by day it increases — and then every strip dissolves into light and the wall reads 為道日損 — day by day it diminishes.
