Folded paper, tier by tier. Each ring turns against the one inside it — measured on the thing this came from, the middle runs one way at half a degree per frame and everything outside it runs the other, with no zoom at all.

The ornament is generated here, not scanned: every petal draws its own. Each time a petal passes the top of its ring it strikes a note, so the tiers with different counts drift in and out of phase with each other and the rhythm is the geometry.
