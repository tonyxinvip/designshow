Kaleidoscope

A kaleidoscope is two mirrors set at an angle. Reflections come in pairs, so the symmetry group is DN, not CN. Drop the mirror step and the figure collapses into a pinwheel. Hard to say what is wrong with it, easy to see that something is.

Life Is an Ocean

A friend’s handwritten letter, in Chinese. Twenty-two lines circle the frame; whichever one reaches the top burns brightest, then spirals inward and falls into the hole at the centre. Every character in the wall behind them is taken from the letter. Change the letter and the wall changes with it.

Armature

Rods, hoops and ball joints sitting at real depths. Fly through it and the near ones sweep past while the far ones barely move. Measured on the sculpture this came from, that ratio is about twenty to one, and it is the whole effect. A flat zoom cannot fake it.
