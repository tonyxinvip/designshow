Nothing here spins. Measured on the source, the rotation between frames is a median of zero degrees and the zoom is zero pixels. What reads as movement is a stack of rings that hold dead still and then jump.

The dome is built, not drawn. Every slat is a six-sided box on a spherical cap that runs into a drum coming down at you, so the near faces are more than twice the size of the far ones and you see the ends and the sides. Twelve-fold: the structure repeats exactly, the light does not. That mismatch is what keeps it from reading as wallpaper.

The sound is built in the page, not a recording. It has no beat, because the picture has none: each jump rings a bell, each inversion breathes.
